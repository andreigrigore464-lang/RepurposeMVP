# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Content creators, founders, and B2B marketing teams repurposing blog posts, articles, RSS feeds, and long-form content into high-converting social carousels and decks.

## Product Purpose
Repurpose MVP transforms long-form content into high-performing social assets (swipeable LinkedIn PDF carousels, Instagram slide decks, and social cards) using automated AI prompt chains and dynamic image/slide generation pipelines.

## Positioning
An AI-powered content transformation engine combining Gemini AI structured prompt chains, dynamic layer-mapped templates (Templated.io), Cloudinary media pipelines, and client-ready PDF generation in a unified creator studio.

## Operating Context
- Web-based creator studio: source intake (URL/RSS/text), AI slide script generation, template & layer mapping, preview & quick editing, and multi-format export/distribution.
- Dashboard workflows with template galleries, live slide previews, resolution engine settings, and batch automations.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Multi-format social asset rendering (LinkedIn PDF carousels, Instagram slide decks, social preview cards).
- External integrations: Google Gemini API, Cloudinary, Templated.io, Unsplash, PostgreSQL/Prisma.
- Tech stack: Next.js 15 (App Router, React 19, TypeScript), TailwindCSS v4, Lucide Icons.

## Brand Commitments
- Personality: Vibrant & expressive creator studio with bold gradients, playful micro-interactions, and high visual craft.
- Visual accents: Modern dark palette complemented by energetic emerald, teal, and violet gradients with glassmorphic cards and tactile hover responses.

## Product Principles
- **Effortless Transformation**: Turn raw content into polished multi-slide assets with minimal manual friction.
- **Expressive Visual Quality**: Deliver slide decks and social assets that feel custom-designed, vibrant, and engaging.
- **High-Clarity Previews**: Provide immediate, pixel-accurate feedback during slide editing and template configuration.
- **Reliable Automation**: Guard density, formatting, and layer resolution heuristics to ensure clean automated outputs.
